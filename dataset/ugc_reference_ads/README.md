# UGC reference ads (metadata only)

21 creator ads (Meta Ad Library downloads supplied by the editor) were studied on 2026-10-24. The videos are other people's ads and are not published; `manifest.csv` records what was measured and seen.

Columns: `hard_cuts_scene0.35` = ffmpeg scene-change count at threshold 0.35 (true cuts); `visual_changes_scene0.12` = count at 0.12 (cuts plus overlay changes), so `duration / visual_changes` is how often the frame changes.

Findings and the five named styles: `../../UGC_REFERENCE_STUDY.md` and `../../UGC_reference_styles_board.png`.
How the findings may and may not be used: `../../instructions/memory/hard-rules-no-slop.md`.
