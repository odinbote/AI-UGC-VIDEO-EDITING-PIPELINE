# UGC reference study — 21 creator ads vs "Normal UGC 1"

Source: Drive folder "UGC References" (Meta Ad Library pulls, facebook/ main set; `_not_ugc_style` and `_duplicates` skipped). All 21 measured for length, resolution and cut rate; 13 read frame by frame.

## 1. What the references do (patterns across the set)

**Length and pacing**
- Most run 60–150 s (one-take talking head + overlays). The short ones (17–32 s) are the fast, overlay-dense ones — same length band as ours.
- Visual change rate splits the set in two: slow talkers change the frame every 5–10 s (newline, Google-coach ads, Lia), fast ones every 1–2 s (Aliyan 1.9 s, Faraz 1.5–2.3 s, "6 weeks" 1.4 s, vibe-coding 1.6 s). The fast group is the one worth copying; "Normal UGC 1" sits at ~2.5 s.

**Cuts**
- Hard jump cuts on the talking head are rare. Instead: the A-roll stays continuous and the *overlay layer* changes (card in, card out, full-screen insert). Scale punches are small (3–8 %) and land on emphasis words, not on every cut.
- Full-screen inserts are 1–3 s, then straight back to the face. Two or three in a row is the max before returning.
- "Rhythm beats" with no words: a 0.5 s blurred freeze of the presenter with a giant number (Hiring Funnel "$125K", "34"), a flash-to-white, or a 1-s stock clip (chalkboard "Career" loop, worker on ladder) between sentences.

**Text**
- Captions are 1–3 words, always ≤ 2 lines, sans-serif bold, white with a black stroke or on a dark pill, placed 0.28–0.40 from the bottom. Keyword in a second colour (orange, yellow, green, red) is standard — ours has no keyword colour.
- Headline cards sit at the TOP (y 0.10–0.20) above the head: white rounded card + brand colour highlight on the key phrase (newline), or giant 2-line numbers ("$200K", "200 Applications").
- Verdict stamps: "OVERRATED" (red) / "UNDERRATED" (green) in a bordered pill with glow, popping in on the word — the cheapest high-impact device in the set.
- Stat counters: a dark card with a label and a huge number that counts up (Interviews 0 → 25; $150k → $200k; credit score ticking 476 → 724).
- Script/serif italic is used sparingly as a *contrast* font (Hiring Funnel: italic script line + bold sans line in the same caption) — same idea as our Instrument Serif + SF Pro pairing.

**Visual placements and overlays**
- Product UI is shown four ways: (a) phone mock held in hand or floating beside the head (Devmaxx, "4K ads"), (b) full-screen phone/laptop UI with the presenter cut out small over it (Grind: circular head bubble on the product screen), (c) split frame — presenter bottom half, screen/card top half (CodeSpring), (d) full-screen UI with motion (score gauge counting, to-do list ticking).
- Diagrams instead of screenshots: Faraz draws the point as a dark-navy motion-graphics scene (ATS gate, 200-dot grid, referral arrows, resume with red strike-throughs) — the strongest look in the set and the one that reads best at 1080 px.
- Testimonial stacks: two or three stacked webcam portraits with name labels (Hiring Funnel, Faraz "Practice live"), used while the voice says "people like X".
- Emoji + icon stickers with pop-in on nouns (Aliyan's cap, puzzle, 700 % bar; "jobs 🏢", "benefits 🏥") — cheap, readable, friendly.
- Stock/b-roll is short and literal: hands typing, worker on ladder, office desks, chalkboard. 1–2 s each, often desaturated or tinted.
- CTA: a dedicated end card (logo, offer, button) 2–3 s, not just a caption.

**Sound (from the edits' rhythm)**: emphasis words carry the pop; hits land on overlays, not on the talking head.

## 2. "Normal UGC 1" vs the references — what to keep, what to fix

Keep (we're already at the top of the set here): continuous A-roll with no jump cuts; real full-frame page recordings with cursor (nobody in the set does this as well); clean cutout; title stacks on the word; click SFX on cuts; full outro.

Improve:
1. **Keyword colour.** Every strong reference highlights one word per caption/title. Add a brand-colour (fanout orange/green) keyword to captions and to the title stack's box line.
2. **Verdict / stat devices.** We have no stamps or counters. Add an "OVERRATED/UNDERRATED"-style stamp preset and a counting-number card ("200 lessons", "16 free", "$200K") for numbers in the script.
3. **Headline card at the top.** The reference's top card (newline style) frees the chin area; use it for section titles while captions stay low.
4. **Rhythm beats.** Insert one 0.5 s freeze + giant number, or a 1 s tinted stock cut, between sections instead of running captions wall-to-wall.
5. **Diagram inserts.** For "how systems at Netflix work", a 2 s dark-navy animated diagram (boxes + arrows, like Faraz's ATS/referral scenes) beats a page scroll. Build 5–6 reusable diagram scenes.
6. **Split-frame layout** (presenter bottom half, card/UI top half, both in rounded frames) as a third layout alongside full-frame and matte+cutout.
7. **Testimonial / social-proof stack** when the script mentions people or companies: stacked portraits or logo rows.
8. **End card** for the CTA (logo, "fanout.sh", button) after the spoken CTA, before the loader.
9. **Emoji/icon pops** on concrete nouns in casual scripts.
10. **Length.** References that work are 60–90 s; our scripts are 20–30 s. Fine for reels, but for ad placements a 60 s version with 2–3 proof beats (numbers, testimonial, diagram) would match the set.

## 3. Five styles to add (named, ready to build as "Style N")

| Name | Reference | Signature | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stamp UGC** (Aliyan) | Get FAANG Internship Offers | Outdoor handheld; every claim gets a verdict stamp (OVERRATED red / UNDERRATED green), stat cards with counting numbers, emoji pops, glowing text; change every 1.9 s | Hot-take / myth-busting scripts |
| **Motion-Graphic Explainer** (Faraz) | Book a free call / 6-Figure SWE | Studio talking head cut against full-screen dark-navy diagram scenes (ATS gate, dot grids, arrows, resume strike-throughs), big 2-line numbers, "STEP 1/2/3" cards, testimonial portrait stacks | "How X works" / process scripts |
| **Top-Card Authority** (newline) | Land a $200K+ AI Job | Static clean frame; white rounded headline card at the top with brand-colour highlight, bold uppercase 2–3 word captions with orange keyword, long takes, branded end card | Expert monologue / webinar-style |
| **Product-Bubble Walkthrough** (Grind / Devmaxx) | LeetCode for system design | Product UI full screen or phone-in-hand, presenter as a circular bubble or small cutout, captions with green keyword on a dark pill, real cursor/typing, end card with URL | Feature demos / product tours |
| **Lo-fi Pop** (vibe coding / Hiring Funnel) | Vibe coding, finally explained; Lia | Laptop-on-desk slides with ✓/✗ emoji cards, pen pointing, or sunny handheld with flash-to-white and blurred freeze + giant number beats, italic-script + bold caption pairing | Casual, funny or story scripts |

"Normal UGC 1" stays the house style; each of the five above is a set of presets (caption look, overlay devices, layout) on top of the same pipeline.
