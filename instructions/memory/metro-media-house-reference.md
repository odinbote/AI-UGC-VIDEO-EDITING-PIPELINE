Abik sent eight @metromedia.house reels on 2026-11-05 (Palmer Luckey / Anduril 90K likes, Palantir Mafia 51K, YC photo 18K, Andrew Mason / Descript, Legora hallway, A24 vs a16z, viral launch, law of category) and asked for "a script like this where I want lots of real clips, plug fanout at the end very subtle".

Format: faceless narrator, third person, 60–110 s. Hook is "This founder / this photo / this man…" plus one impossible-sounding fact (or a cold-open real soundbite). Story runs in time order, one fact per line, a real clip every 1.5–2 s: interviews, old photos on white cards, press headlines with yellow highlight, product screens, logos, a little movie b-roll. Captions are 1–3 white words, centred. A callback line closes the story ("Groupon was his grand vision. Descript was his team's frustration"). The plug is one soft line after the story ("We collected 13 similar stories in one book. Comment X").

How to apply: story about a real person or artefact in AI/engineering, plain words, script delivered in chat with a real-clip note per line ([[script-delivery-in-chat]]), fanout only in the last line. First one written: the "Attention Is All You Need" paper (eight Google authors, all left, Google paid $2.7B to get one back), plug = "explained step by step on fanout.sh". This is a separate style from [[style-normal-ugc-1]] (no presenter); see also [[devmonk-explainer-reference]].

**Correction 2026-11-05 (Abik, on the "Attention paper" script):** "It should be storytelling, organically stitching some stories and real world things. I don't want technical things, avoid motion graphics type of scripts." The paper script was rejected: its subject was an artefact and its clips were diagrams, PDF scrolls and highlighted letters. **How to apply:** the subject is people and events (who, where, what happened next), several short true stories stitched by one thread, every clip real footage or a real photo (interviews, places, news, ceremonies), no diagrams, no screen graphics, no explaining how the technology works. Second attempt: Hinton + Alex + Ilya, the bedroom, the Lake Tahoe auction, OpenAI, the Nobel.
