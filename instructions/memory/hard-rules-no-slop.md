Abik rejected the "Normal UGC 1 + UGC-study devices" cut (2026-10-28): "Looks slop, AI kind of edit." The reference study informs taste; it does NOT license generic ad graphics. Their look is restrained, human, typographic.

**Never do (consolidated from every round):**
1. No generic "AI-edit" overlays: no bold-sans stroked captions, no big white headline cards, no glowing verdict stamps, no emoji badges, no giant-number freeze frames, no stock navy diagram scenes, no flash-to-white, no template end cards. Their design language only: Instrument Serif Italic, SF Pro keyword in the rose box, small green caption pill, white matte, Faded Film grade.
2. Never put text on the face. Titles under the chin (full frame) or above the head (cutout beats); captions never on a screenshot/recording's content area.
3. No visible jump cuts, no hard zooms mid-sentence, no cutout tilt flips except on layout cuts. Never recut a pre-cut A-roll. Dialogue runs to the file's last frame; outro loader plays out.
4. Music −30 dB with the tail fade; never louder. Click SFX before every cut.
5. Screens = real 9:16 browser recordings with the cursor, full frame behind the cutout, pill at 0.385; never synthetic scrolls, static screenshots, or screens shrunk into a corner. Screens must match the spoken words.
6. Cutout must be clean (BiRefNet), sized per presenter, overlapping the bottom of the screen; movie b-roll full screen WITH the cutout and the title stack above the head.
7. Title stacks tight and staggered like the reference (pitch 0.063, left/centre/right), words properly spaced; captions 1–2 words.
8. Content derived from the script (beats, pages, logos), never copied from a previous reel.
9. Preview first; project file only after approval. Deliver as the Copied_ package (.prproj + XML + media + README).
10. No visible duplication (e.g. two caption systems at once).

Decision 2026-10-28: Abik chose **pure Normal UGC 1** — no study devices, not even in-language tweaks. The rejected plan is parked in `_rejected/` (not in the repo); `tools/overlays.py` stays unused.

**If the UGC-study ideas are used at all**, they must be re-expressed in Abik's own language: e.g. a number beat = a title line in Instrument Serif with the number in the rose box; social proof = logo pops (already in the system); "variety" = more real recordings / stacked screenshot slides / list scrolls, not new graphic types. Ask Abik before introducing any new graphic element.
