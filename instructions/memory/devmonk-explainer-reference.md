Shape: lived scene → impossible fact → "How?" → "this was asked in Uber's interview" → naive way breaks → steps 1–3 → trap → trap → step 4 (the visible result) → step 5 (depth: sharding, H3 hexagons) → payoff on something the viewer paid for (surge) → follow + "send to the friend whose cab never gets accepted".
