Before making any video for Abik, ask one question: **"Background type of text, or without background type of text?"** Ask every time, even if the previous video used one of them.

**Why:** Abik, 2026-11-12, after I studied his Reel 6/7 edits: "there was another type of text style in the video, see those and learn it too, and every time I tell you to make a video ask me if I want the background type of text or without background type of text." He uses both and decides per video.

**How to apply:** ask it up front together with anything else that needs his input (AskUserQuestion with the two options), then build with the chosen style:
- **With background:** key phrase SF Pro Semibold/Medium about 90, white, on a green box (about #1C7C08); lead lines Instrument Serif Italic 65. Exemplars in `Fanout Reel 6 - hook A_1.prproj`.
- **Without background:** key phrase SF Pro Bold 110–154 (125.5 typical), white, no box; lead and tail lines Instrument Serif Regular about 68. Exemplars in `Fanout Reel 7 - hook A_1.prproj`.

Full numbers and positions: `EDITING_SYSTEM.md` §21; copies of his projects in `reference/abik edits 2026-11-12/`. See [[abik-edits-reel-6-7-broll-style]], [[style-normal-ugc-1]].
