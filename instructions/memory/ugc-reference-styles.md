Key findings: good references change the frame every 1.5–2.3 s but never jump-cut the head (overlay layer changes instead); captions are 1–3 words with ONE keyword in a brand colour; headline cards sit at the top above the head; verdict stamps (OVERRATED/UNDERRATED) and counting-number cards are the cheapest high-impact devices; product UI shown as phone-in-hand, full-screen UI with a presenter bubble, or split frame; dark-navy animated diagrams beat page scrolls for "how it works"; rhythm beats (0.5 s blurred freeze + giant number, flash to white, 1 s tinted stock) between sections; dedicated end card for the CTA.
