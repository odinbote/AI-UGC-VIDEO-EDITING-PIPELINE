On 2026-10-29 Abik asked me to read five AI Video Bootcamp PDFs (Scriptwriting Frameworks 2026, Ad Strategy Guide, Advertising Psychology Playbook, Hook Library, UGC Script Templates) and learn what should be in an AI UGC ad. The distillation lives in the repo at `/Users/abiknewar/Work/Claude Editing 2/AD_SCRIPT_PLAYBOOK.md` (linked from README). The PDFs themselves are in `~/Downloads/` (third-party, not in the repo).

Core of it: one desire per ad (six permanent desires), matched to the audience's awareness level (5) and the objective (awareness / converting / retargeting); hook in 1–2 s doing curiosity, emotion or value, no product in the first 3 s for native-style; camouflage (look like native content); gradualization (open on a shared belief, big claim last); specificity; show the mechanism; intensify the "after"; stack receipts; kill objections by redefinition; destroy alternatives; sell identity; tone-matched CTA with risk reversal; ~55 s with timecoded beats. Sixteen named frameworks (SPARK, MYTH KILLER, COUNTDOWN, ORIGIN STORY, SHORTCUT, RAW TAKE, PRESSURE COOKER, GRIT, CONVICTION, FACE-OFF, BRIDGE, CLIFFHANGER, WITNESS, DEMO DROP, TIME MACHINE, EDUCATOR), each an acronym of beats.

**Why:** Abik wants the script/strategy side of ads understood, not just the edit. The AVB "camouflage" principle supports their restrained, human look; it does NOT reopen generic ad graphics.

**How to apply:** For every new A-roll + script, run the §0 checklist and the §7 script-intake notes in AD_SCRIPT_PLAYBOOK.md (desire, awareness level, objective, framework, sophistication level) and flag script gaps to Abik before the preview. Map beats onto the existing Normal UGC 1 grammar only (keyword in rose box = the desire/claim word, titles = framework beats, recordings = mechanism, logo pops = receipts, b-roll = intensification). Ad-test variants = different hook takes, never new graphics. See [[hard-rules-no-slop]], [[style-normal-ugc-1]], [[ugc-reference-styles]].
